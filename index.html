SAMS Automation

A private helper that keeps St. Augustine's Parish monthly ministry schedule (greeters, Eucharistic ministers, lectors, altar servers and music) up to date.

What it does

  1. It watches one parish mailbox for schedule-change emails whose subject starts with SAMS-.
  2. Two AI models read each request separately, and a set of parish rules checks the result.
  3. The parish schedule coordinator gets a summary and replies CONFIRM before anything changes.
  4. Only after that confirmation are the affected cells of the parish's schedule spreadsheet updated.

Who can use it

SAMS Automation is not a public service. It runs on one computer for the parish and is signed in to a single Google account. Nobody else can sign in to it or use it.
